Mobile version: touch controls

▶ PLAY

↻ Turn your phone sideways

  • Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left side to move
  • FIRE (big red button): hold to keep shooting, it aims for you
  • NOVA (green button): tap for the special attack
  • MODE / HELP / MUTE and LV 1 … LV 100: at the bottom of the screen
  • Tap the screen once to start (the first load takes about 20 seconds)

Needs an internet connection. On a computer? Use the desktop version (keyboard, mouse or game controller).
