Stratos_squirrel_vs_viper is a free neon arcade duel from Stratos Games. You play the Squirrel, firing laser fans and Nova rings, or the Viper, leading a hunting pack. Every level brings more vipers, and the squirrel's power grows to match, so both sides always have the same total strength. Five modes: play the Squirrel against an AI viper pack (solo or 2-player co-op), play the Viper pack against an AI squirrel (solo or 2-player), or go head-to-head in Squirrel vs Viper. Against the AI, human players get a +10% edge. It is playable free in any desktop or laptop browser, with no install.

How Stratos_squirrel_vs_viper plays

The arena is a neon grid. The Squirrel moves with W A S D and fires lasers where you aim the mouse. Hold the button to keep firing. Its special, the Nova, blasts a full ring of lasers in every direction. The vipers slither in from the edges, spit venom, and fire their own Venom Burst ring. When a squirrel and a viper collide, both take damage.

Kill five vipers and you move up a level. Beat a whole pack and everyone refills before the next pack arrives. Green energy gems appear every few seconds: grab one for +25% attack for 10 seconds. The vipers race you for them too.

Five modes, one fair fight

Solo vs AI: you against the pack

One squirrel against an AI viper pack. The AI dodges your lasers, lunges when it gets close, and goes for the power-up gems. This is the mode for learning the game and chasing a high score.

Dual Squirrel: 2-player co-op

Two squirrels on one keyboard (or two controllers), each facing their own viper pack. Player 1 uses W A S D and the mouse, Player 2 uses the arrow keys, Enter and Right Shift.

Squirrel vs Viper: head-to-head

Player 2 takes control of the lead viper, with the rest of the pack backing them up as AI. If the lead viper falls, control passes to the next one. Beat the whole pack (as the squirrel) or the squirrel (as the viper) to win the round. Both players level up after every round. Player vs player is always exactly equal.

Viper vs AI Squirrel: lead the pack

Switch sides: steer the lead viper with W A S D and spit venom where you aim, while the rest of your pack hunts as AI. The AI squirrel keeps its distance, dodges your venom, fires laser fans and grabs the power gems. Beat it to level up; if your viper falls, you take over the next one in the pack.

Dual Vipers vs AI Squirrels: 2-player viper team

Player 1 and Player 2 each steer a lead viper against two AI squirrels.

How the balance works

Most "hero vs horde" games give the enemies numbers and the hero nothing. Stratos_squirrel_vs_viper keeps it exactly even:

  • Squirrel and viper use the same stats at every level: health, defence, attack, fire rate and special cooldown.
  • Each squirrel faces a pack of 1 viper at levels 1-5, and one more every 5 levels (up to 10 on screen).
  • A squirrel facing N vipers gets N× health, N× defence, fires N laser beams per shot and an N× denser Nova ring, so its total strength matches the whole pack.
  • Power-up gems give the same boost to whoever grabs them, squirrel or viper.
  • Human vs AI: in every mode against the AI, the human player gets +10% health, defence, attack and speed. Player vs player stays exactly equal.

Every attack has its own sound, and the sounds change with each game mode and every few levels. Only attacks make sound, so there's no background noise.

Why we built it

Stratos_squirrel_vs_viper started life as a small home coding project and grew into the Stratos Games arcade game. It was built the studio way: AI-built, human-tested. Every change to the balance, sound and controls was played by a person and adjusted from real play-testing before it shipped.

FAQ

Is Stratos_squirrel_vs_viper free? Yes, completely free, with no ads and nothing to unlock.

Do I need to install anything? No. Open the link in a desktop or laptop browser, click once to start, and it plays. The first load takes about 20 seconds.

Can two people play? Yes. Dual Squirrel is co-op and Squirrel vs Viper is head-to-head, both on the same computer. Share the keyboard, or plug in two game controllers.

Does it work with a game controller? Yes. Controller 1 plays the Squirrel, controller 2 plays Squirrel 2 or the Viper. In the browser, press any button on the controller once so the page detects it.

Can I play it on my phone? Not yet. The game currently needs a keyboard, mouse or controller.

How do I jump to a harder level? Press 1-9 for levels 10-90, 0 for level 100, or click the LV buttons at the bottom of the screen. Press H at any time to see every key.

Who makes Stratos_squirrel_vs_viper? Stratos Games, the indie studio behind BLOXPLODE.
